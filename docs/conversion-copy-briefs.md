# Conversion-Focused MVP Copy Briefs

This document resolves CD-41 by defining concise page-family copy briefs for final CMS content entry. It informs CD-31 without replacing final owner review, legal review, or CMS publishing.

Tone: warm, human, professional, clear, inclusive, and not elitist. The copy should make ballet and structured dance training feel serious, caring, and approachable.

## Shared Message Rules

- Lead with ballet, training, continuity, care, and Granada context.
- Explain ages, levels, benefits, how to start, and the next step.
- Treat RAD as an important trust signal without publishing unverified claims.
- Use schedules and contact as practical conversion paths.
- Avoid generic landing-page hype.
- Do not invent awards, testimonials, teacher qualifications, availability, prices, specific class times, or legal claims.
